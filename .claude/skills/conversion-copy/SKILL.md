# Conversion Copy

## Input (what to accept)

- Existing copy to audit, OR brief for new copy
- Business name, offer, and primary conversion goal
- Target audience (demographics, pain points, motivations)
- Tone of voice: professional / friendly / authoritative / playful / urgent
- Competitor positioning (what makes this offer different)
- Copy type: headline / hero / CTA / email / ad / product description / full page
- Current conversion rate (if known, for benchmarking)

## Analyse (detailed checklist)

### 1. Headline Formulas (evaluate which to use)
- [ ] **Benefit headline** — "Get a Safer Garden in One Day"
- [ ] **How-to headline** — "How to Remove a Dangerous Tree Without the Risk"
- [ ] **Question headline** — "Worried Your Tree Could Fall?"
- [ ] **Social proof headline** — "Trusted by 500+ Homeowners in [City]"
- [ ] **Number headline** — "3 Reasons Our Pruning Costs Less Long-Term"
- [ ] **Negativity/fear headline** — "The Hidden Danger in Your Garden You're Ignoring"
- [ ] **Direct benefit** — "Free Quote. Same-Week Service. Fully Insured."
- [ ] Headline is 6–12 words (optimal for scanning)
- [ ] Headline passes the "so what?" test — benefit is clear immediately

### 2. Value Proposition
- [ ] Answers: What is it? Who is it for? Why this one? Why now?
- [ ] One sentence deliverable: "[Product] helps [audience] achieve [outcome] by [mechanism]"
- [ ] Unique differentiator stated (not "quality service" — too generic)
- [ ] Outcome-focused, not process-focused
- [ ] Passes the "blink test" — understood in under 5 seconds

### 3. CTA Copy
- [ ] Uses first-person language ("Get My Quote" vs "Get Your Quote")
- [ ] Action verb leads ("Get", "Book", "Start", "Claim", not "Submit", "Send", "Click")
- [ ] Specifies what happens next ("Get My Free Quote — We'll Call Within 2 Hours")
- [ ] Reduces perceived risk ("No obligation", "Cancel anytime", "Free")
- [ ] Matches the stage of intent (awareness = soft CTA, decision = hard CTA)
- [ ] Microcopy below CTA addresses hesitation ("No credit card required")
- [ ] Maximum 5 words on the button itself

### 4. Benefit vs Feature Language
- [ ] Every feature has a corresponding "so you can..." benefit
- [ ] Feature: "ISA-certified arborists" → Benefit: "your trees are assessed by qualified professionals, not guesswork"
- [ ] Benefits are specific and visual (paint a picture of the outcome)
- [ ] Avoid jargon unless writing for an expert audience
- [ ] Use sensory and emotional language where appropriate

### 5. Objection Handling
- [ ] Cost objection — addressed with value framing or ROI
- [ ] Trust objection — addressed with credentials, reviews, guarantees
- [ ] Timing objection — addressed with urgency or process clarity
- [ ] "Do it myself" objection — addressed with risk, effort, or expertise gap
- [ ] "Not sure if I need it" objection — addressed with pain amplification
- [ ] Each objection handled exactly once (repetition = desperation)

### 6. Urgency & Scarcity
- [ ] Urgency is TRUE and specific ("Booking 2 weeks out — limited slots remaining")
- [ ] Deadline-based urgency (seasonal, offer end date)
- [ ] Scarcity is legitimate (team capacity, material availability)
- [ ] No fake countdown timers or manufactured scarcity (trust damage)
- [ ] Loss framing used ethically ("Don't let a dead tree become a claim")

### 7. Readability Scoring
- [ ] Average sentence length < 20 words
- [ ] Paragraph length < 4 lines on desktop
- [ ] Flesch Reading Ease: aim > 60 (plain English) for B2C
- [ ] No passive voice where active is possible
- [ ] Contractions used in informal copy (we're, you'll, it's)
- [ ] Short words preferred over long (use, not utilise)
- [ ] Front-loading: most important info first in every sentence

### 8. Microcopy
- [ ] Form field labels clear and specific ("Your mobile number" not "Phone")
- [ ] Placeholder text aids completion (example input, not instruction)
- [ ] Error messages explain how to fix (not just what's wrong)
- [ ] Success messages confirm what happens next ("We'll call you within 2 hours")
- [ ] Button loading state says what's happening ("Sending your request...")
- [ ] Privacy reassurance near email fields ("We never share your details")

## Code Examples

### Headline formula bank (fill in your specifics)
```
Benefit:     "[Specific outcome] in [timeframe]"
             "Get [desirable result] without [common pain]"

How-to:      "How to [achieve goal] even if [common objection]"
             "How [audience] can [benefit] in [timeframe]"

Question:    "Worried about [specific fear]?"
             "Is your [thing] putting [what at risk]?"

Social:      "[Number]+ [audience] in [location] trust us for [service]"
             "Rated [X]/5 by [N] verified customers"

Number:      "[#] reasons [audience] choose [brand] over [alternatives]"
             "[#] things to check before hiring a [service provider]"

Urgency:     "Book before [deadline] — [X] slots remaining this month"
             "Seasonal offer: [benefit] — ends [date]"
```

### Value proposition formula
```
For [target audience]
Who [have this problem/need],
[Product/service name] is a [category]
That [primary benefit].
Unlike [alternative/competitor],
We [key differentiator].
```

**Green Barber example:**
```
For homeowners in [City] worried about tree safety,
Green Barber is a fully insured tree surgery service
that removes dangerous trees safely and quickly.
Unlike general gardeners,
our ISA-certified arborists assess risk before any chainsaw touches your tree.
```

### CTA copy formulas
```
Soft CTAs (awareness/interest stage):
- "See How It Works"
- "Learn About Our Process"
- "Read Customer Stories"

Medium CTAs (consideration stage):
- "Get My Free Estimate"
- "Check Availability"
- "Download the Guide"

Hard CTAs (decision stage):
- "Book My Free Survey"
- "Get My Quote Today"
- "Claim My Discount"
- "Start My Free Trial"

Risk-reducing microcopy (place under CTA button):
- "Free, no-obligation — we'll call within 2 hours"
- "No credit card required"
- "Takes 60 seconds"
- "Join 500+ satisfied customers"
- "Cancel anytime"
```

### Feature-to-benefit transformation
```
Feature                           Benefit
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ISA-certified arborists       →   Your trees are assessed correctly, not guessed
Fully insured (£5M cover)     →   If anything goes wrong, your property is covered
Same-week availability        →   You don't wait weeks while the hazard stays in your garden
Free site assessment          →   No cost, no commitment — you know exactly what's needed before you spend anything
Eco-friendly wood chipping    →   Zero landfill — your garden waste becomes garden mulch
```

### Objection-handling copy blocks
```tsx
// Cost objection
const CostObjection = () => (
  <p className="text-gray-600">
    Tree removal is a one-time cost that prevents years of insurance claims,
    property damage, and rising removal costs if the tree falls first.
    Most customers tell us they wish they'd called sooner.
  </p>
)

// Trust objection
const TrustObjection = () => (
  <ul className="space-y-2 text-gray-600">
    <li>ISA-certified team — not weekend gardeners</li>
    <li>Fully insured to £5,000,000 public liability</li>
    <li>4.9/5 from 200+ verified Google reviews</li>
    <li>Members of the Arboricultural Association</li>
  </ul>
)
```

### Readability check (write to this standard)
```
Target: Flesch Reading Ease > 60 (plain English, accessible to all)

Before (score: 42 — hard to read):
"Our comprehensive arboricultural services encompass a multifaceted
approach to tree management, incorporating evidence-based methodologies
to ensure optimal phytosanitary outcomes."

After (score: 74 — plain English):
"We keep your trees healthy and safe. Our qualified team handles
everything from pruning to full removal — with zero mess left behind."

Rules:
- Sentences: average 15 words or fewer
- Paragraphs: 2–3 sentences max for web
- Words: favour 1–2 syllables where possible
- Voice: active ("We remove the tree" not "The tree will be removed by us")
- Contractions: use them (we're, you'll, it's, they've)
```

### Microcopy patterns
```tsx
// Email field with privacy reassurance
<div className="space-y-1">
  <label htmlFor="email" className="block text-sm font-medium text-gray-700">
    Your email address
  </label>
  <input
    id="email"
    type="email"
    placeholder="you@example.com"
    className="..."
  />
  <p className="text-xs text-gray-500">
    We never share your details. Unsubscribe anytime.
  </p>
</div>

// Form success message
<div role="status" aria-live="polite" className="...">
  <h3>Quote request received!</h3>
  <p>We'll call you within 2 hours during business hours (Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm).</p>
</div>

// Error message (specific and actionable)
<p role="alert" className="text-destructive text-sm">
  Please enter a valid UK mobile number (e.g. 07700 900000)
</p>
```

## Manual Review Required

| Check | Tool / Method | Why Manual |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| Flesch Reading Ease score | Hemingway App / readable.io | Copy-paste and check score |
| 5-second test (headline clarity) | UsabilityHub / real users | Ask "what does this business do?" |
| A/B test results | Google Optimize / VWO | Needs live traffic and time |
| Tone of voice alignment | Brand team review | Subjective brand fit |
| Legal compliance (claims) | Legal review | "Best in [city]", guarantees, stats |
| Emotional resonance | User interviews | Does copy speak their language? |
| CTA click-through rate | Analytics | Requires instrumented tracking |
| Urgency legitimacy | Founder confirmation | Verify scarcity claims are true |
